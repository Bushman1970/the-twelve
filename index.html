The Twelve

Jesus chose and named all twelve (Mark 3:13–19). The Gospels list them in three fixed groups of four — the first name in each never changes, and Judas is always last.

Two sets of brothers

Peter & Andrew, James & John — whole families off the same boats.

Person to person

Andrew brought Peter; Philip brought Nathanael — invitation from day one.

Enemies at one table

Matthew collected taxes for Rome; Simon burned to drive it out.

The inner three

Peter, James & John alone saw the Transfiguration — and were taken deepest into Gethsemane.
