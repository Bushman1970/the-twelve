# The Twelve

An interactive companion for a 12-session small-group study of the twelve disciples — their history, personality, perspective, and contribution. Built to display on a living-room TV during discussion.

**Live page:** https://bushman1970.github.io/the-twelve/

- Tap any disciple to open his session panel
- `←` `→` move between disciples · `↑` `↓` toggle "The man" / "Talk it over" · `Esc` back to the map
- All Scripture quotations are ESV

## Leader's Console

**Live page:** https://bushman1970.github.io/the-twelve/guides/

All twelve sessions in one page, built for the leader rather than the room:

- Click any disciple to open his night, or use `guides/#/1` … `guides/#/12`
- Each session shows the spine of the night, four scenes from the Gospels, the
  Christ-centered guardrail, and the five discussion questions
- **Session clock** — enter the time you actually started and every one of the
  ten beats gets a real wall-clock time; the beat you are in highlights itself
- `←` `→` move between sessions · `Esc` returns to the overview
- Extra-biblical material is flagged *tradition, not Scripture* throughout
